One-tap set-up

With two shortcuts, one tap (or a double-tap on the back of your iPhone) moves the text, without opening this page. You do this once. It takes about five minutes.

Shortcut 1: Get from PC
  1. Tap Copy next to the address above.
  2. Open the Shortcuts app. Tap + (top right).
  3. Tap Search Actions, type Get Contents of URL and tap it.
  4. Tap the word URL in that step, then tap it again and choose Paste. Tap Done on the keyboard.
  5. Search again: type Copy to Clipboard and tap it.
  6. Search again: type Show Notification and tap it. Tap its text (Hello World!), delete it, and type Copied from PC.
  7. Tap the name at the top (with the small arrow), choose Rename, and type Get from PC. Tap Done.
  8. Try it: copy some text on the computer, then tap Get from PC in Shortcuts. The first time, the iPhone asks to connect to your computer: tap Allow (or Always Allow).
Shortcut 2: Send to PC
  1. In Shortcuts, tap +.
  2. Tap Search Actions, type Get Clipboard and tap it.
  3. Search again: type Get Contents of URL and tap it. Tap URL and paste the same address as before (tap Copy above again if needed).
  4. Tap the small arrow at the right of that step to show more options. Tap Method and choose POST.
  5. Tap Request Body and choose JSON. Tap Add new field and choose Text. In Key, type text. Then tap the box next to it (Text) and tap Clipboard in the bar above the keyboard.
  6. Search again: type Show Notification and tap it. Tap its text, delete it, and tap Contents of URL in the bar above the keyboard. (This shows the computer's answer, like "Sent to your computer".)
  7. Tap the name at the top, choose Rename, and type Send to PC. Tap Done.
  8. Try it: copy some text on the iPhone, tap Send to PC, then press Ctrl+V on the computer. If the iPhone asks about pasting or connecting, choose Allow (or Always Allow).

So the iPhone stops asking before every paste: Settings, Apps, Shortcuts, Paste from Other Apps, Allow.

One tap: double-tap the back of the iPhone
  1. Open Settings, Accessibility, Touch, and at the bottom Back Tap.
  2. Tap Double Tap, scroll down to Shortcuts, and choose Get from PC.
  3. Go back, tap Triple Tap, and choose Send to PC.

Now: double-tap the back of the iPhone to get the computer's text, triple-tap to send yours.

Other ways to run them: the Action button (iPhone 15 Pro and newer: Settings, Action Button, Shortcut), a button in Control Center, an icon on the Home Screen, or Siri ("Send to PC").

If a shortcut doesn't work
  • It can't connect, or takes a long time: check that the iPhone is on the same Wi-Fi as the computer, and that the computer is on and awake. Phone Clipboard only works on your own network, not over mobile data.
  • Check Settings, Privacy & Security, Local Network: Shortcuts must be on.
  • It says the key is old: a new key was made on the computer. Scan the code on the computer again, copy the new address here, and paste it into both shortcuts (in place of the old one).
  • It worked before and stopped: the computer's address may have changed. Scan the code on the computer again and compare the address. If it's different, put the new one into both shortcuts.
  • On the computer, the Phone Clipboard page (Start menu: Phone Clipboard) shows what's wrong, if anything.
