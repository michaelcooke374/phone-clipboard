Set up your iPhone (once)

  1. Connect your iPhone to the same Wi-Fi as this computer.
  2. Open the Camera on the iPhone and point it at this code. Tap the link that appears under it.
  3. A page called Phone Clipboard opens on the iPhone. Follow One-tap set-up there to make the two shortcuts.

Every day

Computer to iPhone: copy text here as usual (Ctrl+C). On the iPhone, run Get from PC (or double-tap the back of the iPhone, if you set that up). Then paste on the iPhone.

iPhone to computer: copy text on the iPhone. Run Send to PC (or triple-tap the back). Then paste here with Ctrl+V.

Texts from your iPhone

The last 10, kept until Phone Clipboard stops. Handy if you copied something else on the computer in between.

    Settings

    Phone Clipboard starts by itself when you sign in to Windows and works quietly in the background. Open this page again any time: click Start, type Phone Clipboard, press Enter.

    Make a new key if someone else got your iPhone's link. Old links and shortcuts stop working, so set up the iPhone again with the new code.
